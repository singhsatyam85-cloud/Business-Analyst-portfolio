EdTech Attendance Management System

Professional Case Study & Work Pack Summary · Shiv Satyam Singh

Project Overview

This work pack covers the end-to-end business analysis deliverables for the EdTech Attendance Management System, deployed across 6 educational institutions. The platform replaced legacy paper processes with mobile and web-first student verification workflows.

Business Impact

  • Eliminated 4-6 hours of manual daily administration reporting.
  • Synchronized real-time check-ins for teachers, students, and administration.
  • Optimized course schedule mappings and attendance tracking.
