Professional Narrative

I take products from a vague idea to a live launch. I map customer journeys, write the functional specification, user stories and acceptance criteria the developers build from, and stay with it through UAT and sign off.

I work with data as well as documents. I design SQL reporting frameworks and Power BI dashboards, including ones connected to SAP and Salesforce, and I check the numbers against source data before anyone reports on them.

I build AI products, not just AI slide decks. I recently delivered a live AI marketing platform for a client with eight modules and an autonomous agent that takes a task in plain English, asks its own follow up questions, generates the content and posts it straight to LinkedIn and Twitter. Built and deployed using Antigravity, Claude Code and Codex.

I am based in the UK and open to Business Analyst roles, including ones where AI tools are part of the job.
